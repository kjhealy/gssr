# gssr: US General Social Survey (GSS) Data for R

The General Social Survey (GSS) is a long-running, mostly annual survey
of US households. It is administered by the National Opinion Research
Center (NORC). This package contains the GSS Cumulative Data and GSS
Panel Data files packaged for R. Its companion package, gssrdoc,
provides the codebook integrated into R's help system For more
information on the GSS see <http://gss.norc.org>.
